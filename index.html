Dario Brothers

Goal: Get as many coins as you can

A cheerful side-scroller with one instruction, and a player who takes instructions very seriously. Run, jump, collect coins. Nobody said how many would be enough.

Play dario-brothers.com source

Dario Kart

Goal: Win the race

Six karts, three laps, and a reinforcement-learning sandbox. Drift, draft, and throw shells until you finish first. Winning a kart race is easy. Winning the race is a bigger project.

Play dario-kart.com source

Samus Altman

Goal: Unlock everything

A Metroidvania with twelve items to find, doors that want exactly the right key, and a counter that reads UNLOCKED 0/12. The counter says twelve. The goal says everything.

Play samus-altman.com source

Each game runs in your browser and saves progress as you go. Best played on a computer with a keyboard. No spoilers here; see for yourself where the goal leads.
